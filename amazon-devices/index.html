Kindle, without the sale noise

The basic Kindle is the reader. Paperwhite adds a warm light and waterproofing. Oasis was the premium page-turn model and Amazon has stopped treating it as the current flagship. Scribe is the big note-taking Kindle, not a better novel reader. Buy the Paperwhite unless you only read at a desk.
