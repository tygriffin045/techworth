How we pick

We keep products that solve a repeated job (calls, travel charging, file backup) at a price that still makes sense after the first week. We ignore launch-week hype, invented star scores, and specs the maker does not publish. Verdicts are Worth It, Mixed, or Skip. Pages get refreshed when a successor clearly replaces the pick.

Buying guides

6 min · headphones

Sony WH-1000XM5 vs a cheap ANC pair

The XM5 is worth it if you fly or take long calls and want the mic and the clamp to disappear. A $50 ANC headphone is Mixed at best: it cuts airplane rumble, then fails on voices and comfort after an hour. Skip no-name “studio” headphones that list a fake frequency range and no warranty.

5 min · power

Wall charger vs power bank

A 30W USB-C charger covers a phone and most tablets. Buy the Anker 737 only if you also need to top up a laptop away from an outlet. The brick is about 1.4 lb. That weight is the tradeoff, not a flaw to hide.

Questions people actually ask

Are these tested, or just spec sheets?

Picks are limited to products with published specs and a long return window. Where we have not used a unit ourselves, the verdict says so and stays on documented tradeoffs: weight, port count, codec support, warranty.
